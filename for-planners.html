    Working with auditors while protecting their independence

    A smooth SMSF process depends on good coordination between the fund's accountant, planner and independent auditor. We help organise the accounting records and supporting documents needed for the annual audit.

    The appointed auditor remains independent and responsible for conducting the audit and forming their own professional opinion. We do not direct, influence or interfere with that process.

    Prepare and organise relevant accounting records.
    Provide supporting documents and information requested for the audit.
    Coordinate responses to outstanding audit queries.
    Respect the auditor's independent role and professional judgement.
