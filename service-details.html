    How It Works.

    Getting your SMSF paperwork in order doesn't need to be complicated. We guide you through each step, keep you informed and handle the administration while you stay in control of your fund.

    01

    Tell Us About Your Fund

    Start with a quick conversation or enquiry form. Tell us about your SMSF, its members, investments and what support you need.

    02

    Receive a Clear Fixed Quote

    We review your requirements and provide a straightforward fixed-fee quote, clearly outlining what's included and any additional costs.

    03

    We Collect Your Records

    Once you proceed, we help gather the necessary documents from you, your bank and your previous accountant where applicable.

    04

    We Handle the Paperwork

    We prepare your financial statements, tax return and member records, coordinate the independent audit and manage the required lodgements.
