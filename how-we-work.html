    How We Work

    An SMSF works best when everyone understands their responsibilities. You remain in control of your fund, while each professional takes care of their area of expertise. Every role matters. Our job is to keep the administration organised and work with your other professionals where authorised, so everyone can focus on what they do best.

    01

    You, the Trustee

    The decision maker

    What you do

    Make the decisions about your SMSF and remain legally responsible for managing the fund.

    What you don't do

    Hand your trustee responsibilities to someone else, even when you work with professionals.

    02

    Eminent SMSF Solutions

    Your administration partner

    What we do

    Manage your fund's accounts, tax, records, compliance paperwork and annual audit coordination.

    What we don't do

    Provide financial product or investment advice, or recommend and sell financial products.

    03

    Your Financial Planner

    Your advice professional

    What they do

    Advise on SMSF suitability, rollovers, investment choices and retirement strategy.

    What they don't do

    Prepare your SMSF accounts or lodge your fund's annual tax return.

    04

    Your Independent Auditor

    Your annual independent review

    What they do

    Independently examine your SMSF each year and report on its compliance and financial position.

    What they don't do

    Prepare the accounts they are responsible for independently auditing.

    Good SMSF Administration. With or Without a Planner.

    You don't need to have a financial planner to use our SMSF administration service. At Eminent SMSF Solutions, we take care of the essential accounting, tax, financial reporting and compliance administration required to keep your fund organised and running smoothly. We work with you to manage the paperwork, maintain accurate records and coordinate the annual administration process, while you remain in control of your SMSF and its decisions.

    If you would like professional advice about whether an SMSF is suitable for your circumstances, your investment options or your retirement plans, you can seek guidance from a licensed financial adviser. Our role is to make the administration side simpler, giving you clear information and reliable support throughout the year.

    Your Year with Eminent.

    We follow a clear, structured process from collecting your financial records to preparing accounts, coordinating the annual audit and lodging your tax return, keeping your SMSF organised and on track throughout the year. We keep you informed throughout the process and let you know when information or action is needed.

    01
    GETTING STARTED

    Records Collected

    We gather the relevant financial information and supporting documents for your SMSF, including bank statements, investment records, expenses and other required information.

    02
    ACCOUNTS & REPORTING

    Accounts Prepared

    We prepare your fund's annual financial statements, member records and relevant accounting information based on the documents provided.

    03
    INDEPENDENT REVIEW

    Audit Completed

    We coordinate the annual independent audit and provide the required accounting records to the appointed auditor for their review.

    04
    TAX & COMPLIANCE

    Return Lodged

    Following the required review and audit process, we prepare and arrange lodgement of your SMSF annual return with the ATO, subject to the necessary approvals.

    05
    YEAR-END COMPLETION

    Documents Delivered

    Once the annual work is completed, we provide your fund's relevant financial statements, tax documents and records for your reference and ongoing administration.
