    What We Do. What We Don't.

    We believe in transparency and clearly defined responsibilities when it comes to managing your SMSF. From handling accounts, tax returns and administration to understanding where financial advice and independent auditing fit in, here's what we take care of and where other professionals play their part.

    What We Do

    We take care of the essential administration and compliance tasks that keep your SMSF running smoothly.

    • SMSF accounts and financial records
    • Annual tax returns
    • Member statements
    • Trustee documentation
    • Independent audit coordination

    What We Don't Do

    We maintain clear professional boundaries and leave financial advice and independent auditing to the right professionals.

    • Recommend investments
    • Sell financial products
    • Advise whether you should start an SMSF
    • Conduct your fund's independent audit
