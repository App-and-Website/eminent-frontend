    About SMSFs

    An SMSF is a private superannuation fund managed by its own members, who generally act as individual trustees or directors of a corporate trustee. It allows members to manage their retirement savings while remaining responsible for meeting superannuation and tax obligations.

    Generally, individuals who want greater involvement in managing their superannuation can establish an SMSF. An SMSF can have up to six members, subject to specific trustee and membership requirements.

    Trustees are responsible for managing the fund in members' best interests, maintaining accurate records, following the fund's investment strategy and meeting all legal, reporting and compliance obligations.

    Yes. Every SMSF must undergo an independent annual audit, even if the fund has no transactions or has very few assets. The auditor examines the fund's financial statements and compliance with relevant requirements.

    An SMSF should maintain financial statements, bank records, investment documents, contribution records, member information, tax returns, minutes and other supporting documents required for accounting and compliance.
